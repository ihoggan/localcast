# Supplier: Kettleworth Electrical Supplies

Our wholesaler is Kettleworth Electrical Supplies, on the trading estate in Bidston. Trade account number KES-4471. The account is in the firm's name, and Dave and the Maker can both order on it.

## Counter hours and contact

Trade counter opens 7am to 4:30pm Monday to Friday, and 8am to 12 noon on Saturday. Closed Sunday. Ask for Maureen on the counter; she knows the account and will hold stock behind the desk if you ring ahead. Phone orders before 2pm go out on the next delivery.

## Deliveries

Kettleworth deliver on Tuesdays and Fridays only. Minimum order for free delivery is 150 pounds. Anything under that, someone has to go and collect it from the counter. Deliveries go to the job address if it's written on the order, otherwise they come to the lock-up in Moreton.

## Prices and discounts

We get 12 percent trade discount on cable and accessories, and 8 percent on consumer units (fuse boxes) and RCDs. Copper went up again in September, so twin and earth cable is dearer than it was in the summer. Always ask Maureen about end-of-line stock before ordering new.

## Returns

Unused stock can go back within 30 days with the receipt, in its original packaging. Cut cable can't be returned. Kettleworth charge a 10 percent restocking fee on anything over 100 pounds.

## Other supplier

If Kettleworth haven't got something, the backup is Lomax Trade in Birkenhead, but we have no account there, so it's card only and no discount.
