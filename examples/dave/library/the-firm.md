# The firm

Small firm: Dave and the Maker. Dave does the electrical work; the Maker does the quotes, the invoices and the ordering.

## Rates

Day rate is 280 pounds. Half day is 150 pounds. Callouts are 60 pounds for the first hour. Callbacks on our own work are free. Materials are charged at cost plus 10 percent.

## Paperwork

Every quote goes out by email within three working days of the visit. Invoices are due within 14 days. Certificates and building control paperwork are the Maker's job to chase once Dave has done the work.

## The lock-up

Stock and the spare ladders are kept in the lock-up in Moreton, unit 3. The key is on the red fob. Kettleworth deliveries come there unless the order says otherwise.
